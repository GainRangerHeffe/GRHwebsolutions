How it works

A simple monthly rhythm

  1. Plan. A free consultation to learn your business, customers and goals, then a content calendar for the month.
  2. Create. Graphics and captions made for each platform, ready for your review.
  3. Post and engage. Everything is scheduled at the best times, and comments and messages get answered.
  4. Report. A monthly report shows what worked, what grew and what changes next month.

Social media questions

What does social media management include?

Content strategy and an editorial calendar, custom graphics and copywriting, scheduled posting, community engagement and replies, audience growth tactics, and a monthly analytics report.

Which platforms do you manage?

Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, X and LinkedIn. Most businesses do best focusing on the two or three where their customers actually spend time.

Do you run paid social media ads?

Yes. Facebook and Instagram ad campaigns can be added on top of organic posting, with targeting set up for your area and your ideal customer.

How much does social media management cost?

Monthly plans are priced by how many platforms you need and how often you post. You'll get an exact monthly price after a free 15-minute consultation.

No. GRH creates the graphics and writes the captions. Photos of your real work, team and location help a lot, so Cody will ask for those when you have them.
