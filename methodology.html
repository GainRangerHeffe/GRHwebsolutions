Compliance- and cost-aware

Safe to launch, sensible to run

WCAG-, HIPAA- and GDPR-aware, SOC 2-aligned design patterns. Cost-aware AI infrastructure (token, latency, model selection) is built in from day one when you're shipping AI.

WCAG-aware

Text contrast that meets WCAG AA, visible keyboard focus, 44px tap targets, alt text, skip links and reduced-motion support, all measured before launch.

HIPAA-aware

Health details stay out of plain contact forms and email. When a project needs them, the design uses HIPAA-eligible services and keeps that data out of places it doesn't belong.

GDPR-aware

Collect only what's needed, say why, and keep it safe. No unnecessary trackers, and lead backups are locked away from the public web.

SOC 2-aligned patterns

Secrets kept server-side, least-privilege access, rate limits on every public endpoint, logged changes in version control, and backups before every deploy.

Cost-aware AI, in practice

Every AI feature gets a budget before it gets a prompt. GRH picks the model by the job (a large model to reason, a small fast one to chat), keeps prompts and history short so each reply uses fewer tokens, caps usage per visitor and per day, and keeps answers fast enough that people don't leave. Webster, the assistant on this site, answers for about 1.4¢ per reply.

"Aware" and "aligned" mean the site is designed around these standards. Formal certification or a signed agreement with a hosting provider is a separate step, and Cody will tell you when your project needs one.
