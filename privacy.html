Privacy Policy

What GRH Web Solutions collects, why, and how to have it deleted.

The short version

Last updated: November 12, 2026

GRH Web Solutions is a one-person business run by Cody Hoepfl in Erie, Pennsylvania. When you contact me, I use your information to reply to you and to work on your project. I don't sell it, rent it or trade it. You can ask me to delete it at any time by emailing cody@grhwebsolutions.com.

In this policy, "I", "me" and "GRH Web Solutions" all mean the business. "You" means anyone who visits grhwebsolutions.com or contacts me through it or through my ads.

Information you give me

  • Quote and contact forms. Your name, email address, phone number (optional), the service you're interested in, and your message.
  • Webster, the AI chat assistant. Anything you type into the chat. If you tap "Send this chat to Cody", the full conversation is emailed to me along with any contact details you shared in it.
  • Meta (Facebook and Instagram) lead ads. If you fill out an instant form in one of my ads, Meta sends me the details on that form, usually your name, email address, phone number and your answers to its questions.
  • Calls, texts, email and social messages. Your phone number, email address or profile name, and whatever you send me.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, this site uses cookies and similar technology to understand how it's used and to measure ads:

  • Google Analytics records which pages you visit, how you got here, your general location, device and browser, and actions like tapping call or sending a form.
  • Meta Pixel tells Meta when you visit a page, tap call or text, or send a request. Meta uses this to measure my ads and to show ads to people who may be interested, including people who have visited this site.
  • Server logs. My web host records standard technical details such as IP addresses and browser type to keep the site running and secure. The forms and chat also use a hashed version of your IP address to limit spam.

How I use your information

  • To reply to your request by email, phone or text and to give you a quote.
  • To provide the services you hire me for.
  • To understand which pages and ads bring in customers and to improve the site.
  • To prevent spam and abuse.

I won't add you to a mailing list or send you marketing texts unless you ask me to.

Who your information is shared with

I only share information with the services that run this business:

  • Hostinger hosts this website and sends the form emails.
  • DeepAI processes the messages you type to Webster so it can write replies.
  • Google provides Analytics and the fonts on this site.
  • Meta provides the Meta Pixel and lead ad forms.

Each of these companies handles data under its own privacy policy. I may also share information if the law requires it.

About Webster, the AI assistant

Webster is an AI chatbot, not a person, and it can make mistakes. Please don't share passwords, payment details or other sensitive information in the chat. Nothing is sent to me unless you tap "Send this chat to Cody". When you do, I receive the whole transcript by email.

How long I keep it

I keep contact details and messages for as long as needed to respond to you and, if we work together, for as long as I'm providing services and keeping normal business records. You can ask me to delete them sooner. Google Analytics data is kept according to its retention settings, which are 14 months or less.

Your choices

  • Delete or see your information. Email cody@grhwebsolutions.com or call or text 740-319-2431. Tell me the name, email or phone number you used and I'll take care of it, usually within a few days and always within 30 days.
  • Cookies. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. The site still works without them.
  • Google Analytics. You can opt out with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.
  • Meta ads. You can control how Meta uses website activity for ads in your Facebook ad preferences.
  • Texts and calls. If you'd rather I stop contacting you, just say so or reply STOP to a text.

Depending on where you live, you may have more rights under your state's privacy law, such as the right to correct your data. Email me and I'll honor any such request.

Security

This site uses HTTPS, and the server files that hold form backups are locked from public access. No system is perfectly secure, but I take reasonable steps to protect your information.

Children

This site is for businesses and adults. I don't knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent me information, contact me and I'll delete it.

Changes to this policy

If I change how I handle your information, I'll update this page and the date at the top.
