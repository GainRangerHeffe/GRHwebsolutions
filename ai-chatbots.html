FAQ

AI chatbot questions

Can you add an AI chatbot to my existing website?

Yes. A chatbot can be added to almost any website, whether or not GRH built it. It usually takes a small snippet of code and a short server script.

How do the leads reach me?

When a visitor is ready, the chatbot emails you the whole conversation with their name and phone or email, so you know exactly what they need before you call them back.

Will the chatbot make things up?

It's set up with your services, hours and policies and told to stay on topic. It's clearly labeled as an AI, it won't promise prices or dates you haven't approved, and it hands off to you for anything it can't answer.

What does it cost to run?

Chatbots are priced by project. AI usage costs depend on how much traffic your site gets, and GRH sets daily limits so there are never surprise bills.

What else can you automate?

Lead follow-up texts and emails, appointment reminders, connecting your forms to a CRM or Google Sheet, and content pipelines that draft posts for you to approve.
