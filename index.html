FAQ

Common questions

How long does it take to build a website?

Most sites launch in under two weeks. Larger builds with e-commerce, booking or custom integrations are scoped during your free consultation so you get a clear timeline up front.

Do you use templates or page builders?

No. Every site is custom-designed and coded for your business, mobile-first, fast, and built with an SEO foundation. There is no outsourcing; Cody does the work personally.

What does social media management include?

Content strategy and an editorial calendar, custom graphics and copywriting, scheduled posting, community engagement and replies, audience growth tactics, and monthly analytics reports across Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, X and LinkedIn.

Can you add an AI chatbot or automations to my existing website?

Yes. GRH Web Solutions builds AI chatbots and assistants, automated lead follow-up, and integrations that connect your CRM, email and calendar, whether or not we built your site.

Do you only work with businesses in Erie, PA?

GRH Web Solutions is based in Erie, Pennsylvania and works with businesses anywhere in the United States and worldwide.
