The GRH Method

Built fast with AI. Checked by a person.

Writing code is the quick part now. Knowing what to build, what to leave out, and proving it works on a real phone is where a website succeeds.

Cody makes those calls. AI does the heavy lifting, and every change is tested and approved before it reaches your customers.

You click through your site before it goes live

Every build gets a private preview link, so you see and test the real thing, not a mockup.

You talk to the person building it

No account managers or hand-offs. Cody scopes, builds and answers your texts himself.

Compliance- and cost-aware from day one

WCAG-, HIPAA- and GDPR-aware, SOC 2-aligned design patterns. Cost-aware AI (tokens, latency, model choice) is built in when you're shipping AI.

AI does the heavy lifting, a person checks every line

Built with Claude Code for speed. Every change is reviewed, tested and approved by a human before it ships.

Every page is written for Google and AI answers

Clear titles, real FAQs, schema markup and llms.txt, so search engines and AI assistants can quote you.

Tested at phone and desktop sizes before launch

Contrast, tap targets, keyboard focus and speed are measured with browser tests, not eyeballed.

Everything is versioned and backed up

Every change lives in GitHub, and the live site is backed up before each update, so nothing is ever lost.

FAQ

Common questions

How long does it take to build a website?

Most sites launch in under two weeks. Larger builds with e-commerce, booking or custom integrations are scoped during your free consultation so you get a clear timeline up front.

How much does a website cost?

Every project is quoted individually, but typical ranges are: simple static websites $350–$750; websites that need a server (logins, databases, booking, online stores or a custom back end) $1,000–$5,000+; and iOS and Android apps $2,000–$4,000 each. Call or text 740-319-2431 or use the quote form for an exact price.

Do you use templates or page builders?

No. Every site is custom-designed and coded for your business, mobile-first, fast, and built with an SEO foundation. There is no outsourcing; Cody does the work personally.

What does social media management include?

Content strategy and an editorial calendar, custom graphics and copywriting, scheduled posting, community engagement and replies, audience growth tactics, and monthly analytics reports across Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, X and LinkedIn.

Can you add an AI chatbot or automations to my existing website?

Yes. GRH Web Solutions builds AI chatbots and assistants, automated lead follow-up, and integrations that connect your CRM, email and calendar, whether or not we built your site.

Do you only work with businesses in Erie, PA?

GRH Web Solutions is based in Erie, Pennsylvania and works with businesses anywhere in the United States and worldwide.
